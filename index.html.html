    About us

    Why OriNexStudio exists.

    Many life-saving medicines began as plants. OriNexStudio follows that path in plain language, so students, researchers and curious readers can see the whole story in one place.

    Our mission

    Make the journey from plant to medicine easy to explore, and point people to the best research sources along the way.

    What we offer

    A plant library, topic guides across five stages, and quick links to trusted databases and tools.

    Who it’s for

    Students, early-career researchers and anyone curious about natural products and drug discovery.
